Use `Badge` as the eyebrow pill above a headline, or inline as a status chip.

```jsx
<Badge tone="glass">Vibe-coding • 2026</Badge>
<Badge tone="accent" dot>Vagas abertas</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral" size="sm">SOON</Badge>
```

- `glass` on photographic or glowing backgrounds, `neutral` on flat ink, `accent` when the pill itself is the message.
- Copy inside a badge is sentence case and short — three or four words at most.
