Use `Card` for every grouped block of content — module lists, feature tiles, pricing, FAQ shells.

```jsx
<Card tone="glow" padding="lg" radius="xl">
  <h3>Módulo 05 — Pagamentos</h3>
  <p>Mercado Pago do checkout ao webhook.</p>
</Card>
```

- At most one `glow` card per section; it is the emphasis mechanism that replaces colour-coding.
- `glass` only over a glow or an image. `accent` (full violet fill) is reserved for offer/price blocks.
